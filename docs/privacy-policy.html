Privacy Policy for Awrad

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Package ID: com.othmanshawgan.awrad

Awrad is a serene daily Islamic worship, dhikr, and prayer habit tracker developed by Othman Shawgan. Your privacy is fundamental to the app's design: Awrad works completely offline and keeps your spiritual routine private on your device.

1. Data Collection

Awrad does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports. The app does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not require an account.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All data you create in Awrad — including your awrad tasks, daily notes, prayer logs, reminder settings, and theme preferences — is stored locally on your device using standard platform storage. You can remove all stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

3. Device Permissions

Awrad requests only the permissions needed to deliver local reminders you configure:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display your scheduled daily dhikr and awrad reminder notifications.
  • Exact Alarms & Boot Receiver (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to ensure your local reminders fire at your chosen times and are restored after a device restart.

4. User-Initiated Sharing

If you choose to share a progress report from the Progress tab, Awrad uses your device's native share sheet to pass the text you selected to the app you choose. No data is sent automatically.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Awrad collects no personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce optional features that affect data handling (such as optional cloud sync), this Privacy Policy will be updated at this URL prior to releasing those features.
